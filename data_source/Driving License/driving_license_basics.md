## What Is a Driving License?

A Nepali Driving License — officially called Savari Chalak Anumati Patra — is a government-issued document that legally authorizes you to operate a specified category of motor vehicle on public roads in Nepal.

Issued by the Department of Transport Management (DoTM) under Nepal's Provincial Government framework. Nepal transitioned to smart card-based digital licenses in 2015. The current smart card includes a multi-layer QR code for verification.

> Driving without a valid license is illegal under Section 25 of the Motor Vehicles and Transport Management Act 2049 and can result in fines, vehicle impoundment, and legal penalties.

- Original Citizenship Certificate (Nagarikta) — front and back (scanned/photographed, each file under 1 MB)
- Mobile number registered in your name (this number appears on your license)
- Email address (optional but recommended)
- Blood group information (if available; can be obtained at the transport office for NPR 50)

## Application Process — Step by Step

**Step 1 — Open the DoTM Portal**
- Go to: applydl.dotm.gov.np (or your province's portal)
- Use Google Chrome for best compatibility
- Portal available: Sunday–Wednesday, 6:00 AM–10:00 PM only

**Step 2 — Enter Mobile Number and OTP**
- Enter your mobile number and complete the CAPTCHA
- A 6-digit M-PIN (OTP) is sent to your mobile by SMS
- Save this M-PIN — it is reusable for all future logins
- Use "Forgot M-PIN" if you lose it

**Step 3 — Select Your License Status**
- "No, I don't have a Nepali Driving License" — first-time applicants
- "Yes, I have a Nepali Driving License" — adding a new vehicle category
- "No License but gave biometrics before disruption" — previously interrupted applicants

**Step 4 — Fill Citizenship Details (English)**
- Enter all details exactly as on your Citizenship Certificate
- Key fields: Full name in English, Date of Birth (B.S.), Citizenship Number (with all dashes/slashes), Issue District, Issue Date
- If you see "Details not found from Citizenship Information Management System" — this is a known system issue; click Next and continue

**Step 5 — Fill Applicant Details (Devanagari)**
- Enter full name in Nepali script
- Mandatory: Gender, Blood Group, Occupation, Education Level
- Optional: Huliya (physical description), Training Institute

**Step 6 — Guardian and Address Information**
- Select guardian relationship (Father is most common) and enter full name in English
- Enter permanent address exactly as on citizenship: Province, District, Municipality, Ward Number, Tole
- If current address differs from permanent address, enter separately

**Step 7 — Upload Citizenship Photos**
- Upload front of citizenship (under 1 MB)
- Upload back of citizenship (under 1 MB)

**Step 8 — Review and Submit**
- Review every field carefully — last chance for online corrections
- Tick declaration checkbox and click Submit
- Note your Reference Number and take a screenshot
- Form is locked after submission — corrections at office cost NPR 400–500

**Step 9 — Select Category, Office, and Date**
- Click "Apply for New License" from your dashboard
- Select health checkup at Transport Management Office (recommended)
- Select vehicle category, nearest transport office, and available appointment date

**Step 10 — Download and Print Application**
- Click "Download Application" to generate a PDF
- Print on A4 paper
- Bring to transport office on your appointment date

## Biometric Day — What to Expect

Arrive early — applicants who arrive by 7–8 AM are served first.

Before joining any queue: photocopy your application form, citizenship, and blood group card; staple together and attach a NPR 10 postal stamp.

**1. Biometric Registration**
Fingerprints (both thumbs), signature, and photograph captured in the DoTM system. You receive an Application ID on a data record slip. Re-applicants or Add Category applicants do not repeat full biometrics if already in the system.

**2. Medical Examination**
- Long Distance Vision Test: conducted at 20 metres — identify direction of the letter "E" per eye
- Colour Vision Test: identify numbers in a colour-coded book
- Prescription glasses are accepted for the vision test
- If you fail in-office, an external doctor's certificate from a nearby eye clinic is generally accepted

**3. Revenue Payment**
Pay NPR 500 government fee at the counter. Keep the receipt — required on written exam day and trial day.

**Documents to Bring:**
- Printed online application form (A4)
- Original citizenship certificate (not a photocopy)
- Blood group card (or get one at office for NPR 50)
- Prescription glasses (if applicable)
- For Add Category: original existing driving license (or police report if lost)

|---|---|

## Renewal

- Renew online at applydl.dotm.gov.np in the final year of validity
- No written exam or trial exam required for standard renewals
- Biometric update required at transport office appointment
- Renewed smart card issued within 7–15 working days
- Grace period: 35 days after expiry — no penalty fee
- After grace period: NPR 200 per year of delay added
- If expired for more than 5 years: may need to re-sit the written exam

**Documents for Renewal:**
- Original existing driving license
- Original citizenship certificate
- Online renewal application

---

## Duplicate / Lost License

- Apply online at applydl.dotm.gov.np
- No written exam, trial exam, or fresh biometric/medical test required
- Fee: NPR 500
- Submit: police report + original citizenship + application

## Emergency / Fast-Print License

- For urgent needs: travel abroad, employment, peacekeeping missions
- Smart card ready within 3 working days
- Must be applied by the license holder directly using their registered mobile number
- Third-party application is not permitted

---

## International Driving Permit (IDP)

- Allows driving in up to 190 countries with your valid Nepal driving license
- Always carry both IDP and original Nepal driving license simultaneously
- Valid for tourism, travel, and student visa purposes
- Not automatically valid for employment-based driving abroad
- Available in 1-year, 2-year, and 3-year validity options
- Apply at the DoTM office

## Important Notes

- You are NOT restricted to applying in your home district — any DoTM office across Nepal accepts applications
- The online form is available Sunday to Wednesday only, 6:00 AM–10:00 PM — slots fill quickly
- Auto-fill your form by scanning the QR code in your National ID or Nagarik App
- You CANNOT change your transport office after submitting the form
- Keep ALL receipts — required on written exam day and trial day
- Driving on an expired license, even within the 35-day grace period, is technically illegal under Nepal traffic law
