# Driving License Application Form Guide — Nepal

---

## Overview

The driving license application is completed entirely online at applydl.dotm.gov.np. No physical form needs to be filled manually — you fill the form on screen, then download and print the PDF to bring to the transport office.

**Portal availability:** Sunday to Wednesday only · 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Nepal Standard Time)

**Auto-fill tip:** Scan the QR code in your National ID or Nagarik App to auto-fill your personal details instantly — no manual typing needed.

---

## Form Field Reference

| # | Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mobile Number** | Your mobile number registered in your name — this number is printed on your license and used for all notifications |
| 2 | **M-PIN (OTP)** | 6-digit code sent to your mobile via SMS — save it, it is reusable for all future portal logins |
| 3 | **License Status** | Select: "No, I don't have a Nepali Driving License" (first-time) / "Yes, I have a Nepali Driving License" (adding category) / "No License but gave biometrics before disruption" |
| 4 | **Full Name (English)** | Exactly as printed on your Citizenship Certificate — surname first, then given name |
| 5 | **Date of Birth (B.S.)** | In Bikram Sambat format — from your citizenship certificate |
| 6 | **Citizenship Number** | Full number from citizenship including all dashes and slashes — do not abbreviate |
| 7 | **Issue District** | District where your citizenship was issued |
| 8 | **Issue Date** | Date your citizenship was issued (in B.S.) |
| 9 | **Full Name (Devanagari)** | Your full name in Nepali script — use Google Input Tools or phone's Nepali keyboard |
| 10 | **Gender** | Male / Female / Other — as per citizenship |
| 11 | **Blood Group** | Enter if known; if not, it can be recorded at the transport office for NPR 50 |
| 12 | **Occupation** | Your current occupation (mandatory) |
| 13 | **Education Level** | Your highest qualification (mandatory) |
| 14 | **Huliya (Identity Mark)** | Optional — physical description or distinguishing mark |
| 15 | **Training Institute** | Optional — name of driving school if attended |
| 16 | **Guardian Relationship** | Select relationship (Father is most common) |
| 17 | **Guardian Name (English)** | Guardian's full name in English |
| 18 | **Permanent Address** | Province, District, Municipality, Ward Number, Tole — exactly as on citizenship certificate |
| 19 | **Current Address** | Enter separately only if different from permanent address |
| 20 | **Email Address** | Optional but recommended — used for notifications |
| 21 | **Citizenship Photo (Front)** | Scanned or photographed image of front of citizenship — file size under 1 MB |
| 22 | **Citizenship Photo (Back)** | Scanned or photographed image of back of citizenship — file size under 1 MB |
| 23 | **Vehicle Category** | Select from: A / B / C / D / E / F / G / J / K (see category table in driving_license_basics) |
| 24 | **Health Checkup Location** | Select "I want to do health checkup in Transport Management Office" (recommended) |
| 25 | **Transport Office** | Select your nearest or preferred DoTM transport office |
| 26 | **Appointment Date** | Select from available dates shown — slots fill quickly |

## Name Entry Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Use exact spelling from citizenship | Must match exactly — including all surnames |
| English and Devanagari must match | Both versions of your name must be identical in meaning and spelling |
| No titles | Do not write Mr., Ms., Dr., or similar |
| Citizenship number — include all punctuation | Enter every dash (-) and slash (/) exactly as printed on your citizenship |

---

## Document Upload Rules

| Document | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Citizenship Certificate Front | Clear scan or photograph — file size under 1 MB — original document only |
| Citizenship Certificate Back | Clear scan or photograph — file size under 1 MB — original document only |
| Format accepted | JPEG / PNG image |
| Quality | Sharp, fully visible, all text readable — not blurred or cut off |

---

## Date Format

The DoTM form requires dates in **Bikram Sambat (B.S.)** format — the Nepali calendar.

- Your citizenship certificate shows B.S. dates — use these directly
- If you need to convert to A.D. for reference, use an online B.S.–A.D. calendar converter
- Do NOT enter A.D. dates in fields that ask for B.S.

---

## After Submitting the Form

1. A "Download Application" button appears on your dashboard
2. Click to generate the PDF immediately — download and save it
3. Print the PDF on A4 paper (or save as PDF on phone — do not use a screenshot)
4. Bring the printed form to the transport office on your appointment date
5. Also note your Reference Number from the dashboard

---

## Corrections Policy

| Stage | Can You Correct? |
|---|---|
| During form filling (before submission) | Yes — edit any field freely |
| After submission, before appointment | Visit transport office — officer cancels entry and issues corrected form + new date; fee: ~NPR 400–500 |
| After smart card is printed | Correction requires a new card — visit transport office with evidence of correct details |

---

## What to Bring on Appointment Day (Biometric Day)

Based on the information entered in the form, bring these originals:

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Printed application form (A4) | Downloaded from applydl.dotm.gov.np after submission |
| Original Citizenship Certificate | Not a photocopy — original required for verification |
| Blood group card | If unavailable, get one at office for NPR 50 |
| Prescription glasses | Only if you wear vision correction — accepted for eye test |
| Existing driving license | Only for Add Category applicants |
| Police report | Only if existing license is lost |
| NPR 10 postal stamp | Attach to your photocopied document bundle |

Before joining any queue: photocopy application form + citizenship + blood group card, staple together, and attach the postal stamp.

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] All fields filled with information from citizenship certificate
- [ ] Name matches citizenship exactly (both English and Devanagari)
- [ ] Citizenship number includes all dashes and slashes
- [ ] Date of Birth entered in B.S. format
- [ ] Permanent address matches citizenship exactly
- [ ] Citizenship front and back photos uploaded (each under 1 MB, clear and fully readable)
- [ ] Vehicle category selected correctly (check age requirement)
- [ ] Correct transport office selected (cannot be changed after submission)
- [ ] Available appointment date selected
- [ ] Reference Number noted and application PDF downloaded and saved
- [ ] Application PDF printed on A4 paper (or saved as PDF on phone)

---

## Important Tips

- Keep your citizenship certificate in front of you throughout the entire form-filling process
- Do not refresh the page during form submission — you may lose your session
- The portal is only open Sunday–Wednesday — if you miss the window, wait for the next open day
- Appointment slots fill up quickly, especially in Kathmandu — apply as soon as the portal opens
- If the portal shows no available slots for your preferred office, check again on the next open day or choose a different office
- The M-PIN sent to your mobile is your permanent login credential — save it immediately
