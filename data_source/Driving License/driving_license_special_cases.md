# Driving License Special Cases — Nepal

---

## Overview

Certain situations require a different process or extra steps. Use this document to find your specific case.

---

## Special Case Quick Reference

| Situation | What's Different |
|---|---|
| First-time applicant (no prior license) | Full process: online form, biometric, medical, written exam, trial |
| Adding a new vehicle category | No written exam (usually), trial exam required, no fresh biometric/medical |
| Renewal | No written/trial exam, biometric update needed, 7–15 days for new card |
| Lost or stolen license | Online form, NPR 500 fee, police report, no exam or biometric needed |
| Damaged license | Same as duplicate process |
| License expired less than 5 years | Standard renewal — no exam required |
| License expired more than 5 years | May need to re-sit the written exam |
| Urgent / emergency license needed | Fast-Print service — card ready in 3 working days |
| Applying from a different province | Any DoTM office accepted — not restricted to home district |
| Applying abroad | Not available — must apply in Nepal in person |

## Adding a New Vehicle Category

If you already hold a license and want to add another vehicle type (e.g., you have Category A and want to add Category B):

**Process:**
1. Log in to applydl.dotm.gov.np with your existing M-PIN
2. Select "Yes, I have a Nepali Driving License"
3. Select the new category you want to add
4. Book an appointment at your chosen transport office
5. Visit on appointment day with original existing license + original citizenship
6. No fresh biometric or medical test required (if already in system)
7. Written exam is typically waived when adding a related category (e.g., B to A or A to B)
8. Trial exam for the new category is required
9. Pay the applicable government fee for the new category

> If your existing license is lost, bring a police report instead of the original license card.

## Renewal — Detailed Process

**Eligibility:** Renew in the final year of validity (before expiry).

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Log in to applydl.dotm.gov.np
2. Select renewal option
3. Pay government fee online
4. Book biometric update appointment at transport office
5. Visit office with: original driving license + original citizenship
6. Biometric update (photo and fingerprint) is recorded
7. Receive renewed smart card within 7–15 working days

**No written exam, no trial exam required for standard renewals.**

**Late Renewal Penalties:**
| Delay | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Within 35 days after expiry | No penalty — grace period |
| After 35 days, within 5 years | NPR 200 per year of delay added to renewal fee |
| Expired more than 5 years | May be required to re-sit the written exam; process similar to new application |

## Duplicate License (Lost / Stolen / Damaged)

**Process:**
1. File a police report for lost/stolen card
2. Apply online at applydl.dotm.gov.np — select duplicate option
3. Pay NPR 500 fee online
4. Book appointment at transport office
5. Visit with: police report + original citizenship + application
6. No written exam, trial exam, or fresh biometric/medical test required
7. New smart card issued

> If your license is damaged but you are also due for renewal, complete the renewal first — DoTM will issue a new card as part of the renewal without needing a separate duplicate application.

---

## Emergency / Fast-Print License

For urgent situations: international travel, foreign employment, peacekeeping missions.

**Conditions:**
- Must be applied directly by the license holder from their own registered mobile account
- Third-party applications are not permitted
- Smart card ready within 3 working days

**How to apply:**
- Log in to applydl.dotm.gov.np
- Select the emergency/fast-print option
- Pay the applicable fast-print fee
- Visit the designated transport office as directed

## Failed Trial Exam — Retrial Process

If you fail the practical trial test:

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Visit the same transport office where you applied
2. Request a retrial application form at the reception
3. Bring your failed trial slip (or citizenship if slip is unavailable)
4. Fill the bank voucher and pay NPR 500 retrial fee
5. Keep the payment slip — needed for the retrial
6. Receive an admission card with your retrial date (date may not be printed — confirm with the officer)

**Retrial Rules:**
- Maximum 3 attempts within 18 months from biometric day
- After 3 failed attempts: wait 3 months, then restart from the beginning (application, biometrics, written test, trial)
- No written exam retrial needed — only the practical trial

## License Correction (Error in Issued License)

If there is an error in your online application or issued smart card:

**Before your biometric appointment:**
- Log in to the portal and correct the form before submission (form is editable)

**After form submission but before biometric day:**
- Visit the transport office on or before your appointment date
- Inform the officer of the specific error
- Officer cancels the existing form entry in the system
- A corrected form is created and a new appointment date (usually 1–2 days later) is issued
- Correction fee: approximately NPR 400–500

**After smart card is issued:**
- Visit the transport office with original citizenship and evidence of the correct information
- A new card will need to be issued

## Applying from a Different District / Province

You are NOT restricted to your home district:
- Apply at any DoTM transport office across Nepal
- Choose the province portal matching where you will take your tests
- You cannot change your selected transport office after form submission

---

## Applying for Both Category A and B Simultaneously

Many applicants apply for Category A (motorcycle) and Category B (car) at the same time to:
- Complete both written exams in one visit
- Complete both trial tests in one visit (separate tokens required)
- Save time and reduce multiple trips

Both categories are selected during the online form at Step 9. Separate government fees apply for each category.

## Commercial Vehicle License (Categories C, D, E)

For commercial vehicle categories (tempo, truck, bus, lorry):
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Standard process applies (online form, biometric, medical, written exam, trial)
- Government fee: NPR 500 per category
- For Category E (bus/truck): higher responsibility — driving record may be reviewed

---

## Heavy Equipment License (Categories J, K)

For excavators, backhoes, dozers, forklifts:
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Government fee: NPR 3,000 (Category J1/J2, JCB) or NPR 500 (Category K)
- Standard application process applies
- Practical trial conducted for the specific equipment type

---

## License Verification (Checking if a License is Genuine)

To verify whether a Nepal driving license is genuine:
1. Visit dotm.gov.np
2. Navigate to "Smart Card License Details Search"
3. Enter the license holder's Date of Birth and License Number
4. Click Search
5. If correct details appear — license is genuine
6. If no details appear — visit the nearest transport management office immediately

## Nepal Driving License Abroad (International Use)

**Short-term / Tourism:**
- Nepal driving license is generally recognized for tourism and short-term driving in many countries
- Obtain an International Driving Permit (IDP) before traveling for wider recognition

**Long-term / Employment:**
- An IDP alone may not be sufficient for employment-based driving
- Most countries require conversion of your Nepal license to a local license
- Conversion requirements vary by country — check destination country rules

**Example — Finland:**
- Nepal is not on Finland's direct conversion list
- A valid Nepal driving license allows driving in Finland for up to 1 year from arrival
- After that, you must pass Finnish theory and practical exams to get a local license
- Having a Nepal license saves approximately EUR 1,500–2,000 in driving school costs compared to starting from scratch

**IDP Application:**
- Apply at the DoTM office
- Available in 1-year, 2-year, and 3-year validity
- Always carry both IDP and original Nepal driving license simultaneously

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | How to Avoid |
|---|---|
| Citizenship number entered incorrectly (missing dashes/slashes) | Keep citizenship in hand throughout form filling |
| Name mismatch between English and Devanagari fields | Double-check both entries match your citizenship exactly |
| Wrong date format (A.D. instead of B.S.) | Use a B.S. to A.D. converter; DoTM form requires B.S. |
| Selecting wrong transport office | Choose carefully — cannot be changed after submission |
| Not downloading and printing application PDF | Portal is only open Sunday–Wednesday; download immediately after submission |
| Forgetting M-PIN | Save it immediately after receiving; use "Forgot M-PIN" to reset |
| Not bringing all documents on biometric day | Checklist: printed form, original citizenship, blood group card, glasses |
| Driving on an expired license within grace period | Still technically illegal — renew before expiry |
