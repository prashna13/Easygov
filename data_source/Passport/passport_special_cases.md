# Nepal Passport — Special Cases

> Source of record: Department of Passports (DoPassport) official process/FAQ pages — https://nepalpassport.gov.np/process/ (fetched September 2026). Points not directly confirmed on the official site are marked "(secondary source)" and should be verified by a user against DoPassport or the Passport Act 2076 / Passport Rules 2077 (lawcommission.gov.np) before being treated as final.

## 1. Minors (Under 18)

- **National ID (NIN) is not mandatory** for applicants under 16 (unlike adults/16+ applying inside Nepal).
- **(Secondary source)** General guidance from legal/notary guides on minor documentation:
  - Birth certificate (original + photocopy).
  - Both parents' citizenship certificates (original + photocopy).
  - Both parents are generally expected to appear in person at biometric enrolment; where only one parent is available, sole-guardianship or equivalent court documentation, or the other parent's death certificate, may be required.
  - A recommendation letter from the local ward office may be requested for child identification.
  - **Always confirm the current exact document list with the specific enrolment centre**, since minor documentation is one of the more centre-specific processes.
- **Official fee tiers apply by age band** (confirmed directly on nepalpassport.gov.np's fee page):
  - **Under 10 years**, new/renewal: NPR 9,500 (34-page) / NPR 14,500 (66-page).
  - **Under 10 years**, lost/damaged: NPR 14,500 (34-page) / NPR 19,500 (66-page).
  - **10 years and above** (including 10–17-year-olds) fall under the standard adult fee tier: NPR 12,000 (34-page new/renewal) up to NPR 25,000 (66-page lost/damaged) — see `passport_basics.md` §9 for the full table.
- DoPassport has a dedicated FAQ entry on whether children, elderly, or people with disabilities follow a different fee/process ("के बालबच्चा बुढाबुढी अशक्त लाई राहदानी दिँदा छुट्टै प्रक्रिया छ यसको लागि दस्तुर समान हो। कि होइन") — direct users to their enrolment centre for any centre-specific accommodation.

## 2. Children Born Abroad

- DoPassport has dedicated official FAQ entries for this exact situation:
  - "विदेशमा रहेको बखत बच्चा जन्मिएमा राहदानी बनाउन के गर्ने?" (What to do for a passport if a child is born while abroad)
  - "विदेशमा जन्मिएका नवजात शिशुको जन्म दर्ता/अस्थायी नम्बर लिन के गर्नेः" (Birth registration / temporary number for a newborn born abroad)
  - "विदेशमा जन्मिएका सन्तानको राहदानी आवेदन व्यवस्था" (Passport application arrangement for children born abroad)
- **Recommended chatbot handling:** the exact steps depend on birth registration first (via the relevant Nepali mission or the National ID/Civil Registration Department), then the passport application. Direct the user to contact the nearest **Nepali Embassy/Consulate** (for birth registration/citizenship steps) and then follow the normal minor passport process. Do not guess at exact document lists for this case — recommend the user call the mission directly, since procedures can depend on the host country's own birth-registration rules.

## 3. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Passport

1. **Report/cancel immediately.** Apply at the nearest enrolment centre with your identification to have the lost/stolen passport **cancelled**, to prevent misuse. DoPassport explicitly warns: *"A passport reported as lost or stolen must not be used for travel, even if it is later recovered."*
2. **Apply fresh.** A replacement requires a **completely new application** — the same 4-stage process as a first-time passport (see `passport_form_guide.md`).
3. **Fee:** NPR 17,000 (34-page) / NPR 25,000 (66-page) for lost/damaged, age 10+; NPR 14,500 (34-page) / NPR 19,500 (66-page) for a lost/damaged passport belonging to a child under 10.
4. **No copy of the lost passport?** DoPassport has a specific process for this ("मेरो राहदानी हरायो, मसँग प्रतिलिपि पनि छैन...", "हराएको वा झुत्रो राहदानीको अभावमा राहदानी आवेदन प्रक्रिया") — you can still apply; bring your citizenship certificate and explain the situation at the enrolment centre, which can typically retrieve your prior record.
5. **If the "lost" passport is later found**, it has already been cancelled and generally cannot simply be reused — DoPassport has a dedicated FAQ entry on this exact scenario ("हराएको राहदानीको सट्टा नयाँ राहदानी बनाइसकेपछि पुरानो राहदानी भेटियो, के गर्ने?"); contact your enrolment centre for the correct handling.
6. **(Secondary source)** For loss abroad, some legal guides describe filing a police report (FIR) and a newspaper notice as steps under Passport Rule 20 — this level of detail was not independently confirmed on the DoPassport site pages reviewed; treat it as a common practice to mention cautiously, and advise the user to confirm the current requirement with their enrolment centre or embassy.

## 4. Renewal

- Renewal follows the **same 4-stage application process** as a new passport (pre-enrolment → registration/payment/biometrics → notification → collection).
- **When to renew:** DoPassport's official FAQ addresses this directly ("राहदानी नवीकरण कहिले गर्ने?"). As general guidance drawn from the MRP-validity FAQ: renewal becomes advisable once **less than one year of validity remains**, or immediately if the passport is **damaged, full, or has a printing error**.
- **Old passport number / existing visas:** DoPassport has FAQ entries specifically on whether renewal keeps your old passport number and what happens to visas already stamped in the old book ("के राहदानी नवीकरण गर्दा पुरानो सङ्ख्या (Number) कै राहदानी बन्दछ? मेरो राहदानीमा भिसा छ, नवीकरण गर्दा के हुन्छ?"). If you have a valid visa in your current passport, **raise this at the counter before renewal** — enrolment centres can typically preserve the old (cancelled) booklet alongside the new one so the visa remains usable as evidence.
- **Fee:** same as a new passport — NPR 12,000 (34-page) / NPR 20,000 (66-page) for age 10+.

## 5. MRP (Machine-Readable, pre-2021) Passport Holders

- A valid, unexpired MRP **remains usable for travel** — switching to an e-passport is **not required** while it's still valid.
- Apply for a new e-passport once: **under one year of validity remains**, the MRP is **lost/torn/burnt/damaged**, has an **error**, or its **pages are full**.

## 6. Official / Service and Diplomatic Passports

- These are separate categories from the ordinary passport (Passport Act 2076, Section 3).
- **Official/Service passport:** for government employees/officials travelling on deputation.
- **Diplomatic passport:** for diplomats/officials nominated by the Government of Nepal.
- **Document requirements are set by the sponsoring ministry, constitutional body, or office** — DoPassport does not publish a universal checklist and instead refers applicants to that sponsoring authority. DoPassport does provide a downloadable reference document ("Documents required for Official, Service and Diplomatic Passport") from its official file server, linked from the FAQ page.
- **Recommended chatbot handling:** don't guess a document list for this category — advise the user to check with their ministry/office's administration section, since the requirement is set there, not by DoPassport directly.

## 7. Temporary (Handwritten) Passport

- Available **only through a Nepali mission abroad**, for someone who has already applied for an e-passport but urgently needs travel documentation before it's ready.
- It is **handwritten**, and generally **cannot be used for ordinary international travel**.
- In some countries, it **can** be used to apply for a visa, and it can be used to **return to Nepal** — but whether a given destination accepts it should be confirmed with that country's authorities first.
- **A relative cannot "rush" or collect a passport in Nepal on your behalf while you're urgently abroad** — the temporary passport (or, if you have no valid passport at all, a **One Way Travel Document** to return to Nepal) is the official route.

## 8. National ID (NIN) Nuances

- **Mandatory** for ordinary-passport applicants aged 16+ applying from **within Nepal**.
- **Not mandatory** for applicants **under 16**, or for **any age applying from a Nepali mission abroad**.
- DoPassport has a related FAQ on whether a National ID can be obtained *through* an embassy ("विद्युतीय राहदानीका लागि राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र अनिवार्य हो? के दूतावासबाट राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र बनाउन सकिन्छ?") — for NID-specific queries abroad, direct users to the mission or to the National ID Card and Civil Registration Department (donidcr.gov.np).

## 9. Printing/Data Errors After Issuance

- If your **printed** passport contains an error (wrong name spelling, date of birth, etc.), you must apply for a **fresh passport through the full process again** and pay the applicable fee — there is no separate "correction-only" fast path once the booklet is printed.
- If the error was **DoPassport's own mistake** ("Official Error"), the replacement fee tier is explicitly listed as **free** on the official fee schedule.

## 10. (Secondary source) Offences Under the Passport Act, 2076

*Not independently confirmed word-for-word on the DoPassport site pages reviewed — sourced from legal-guide summaries that cite lawcommission.gov.np. Present cautiously and recommend verification against the Act itself for anything with legal consequences.*

| Offence | Note |
|---|---|
| Forging or altering a passport | Reported as carrying imprisonment and a fine under the Act |
| Holding two valid Nepali passports simultaneously | Reported as an offence — both books can be cancelled |
| Using another person's passport | Reported as an offence |
| Giving false information on a passport application | Reported as an offence |

For a chatbot: treat detailed penalty figures (years of imprisonment, exact fine amounts) as **not verified against the primary legal text** in this file, and, if a user needs the exact figures, direct them to the Passport Act 2076 / Passport Rules 2077 at **lawcommission.gov.np**, or to DoPassport directly.

## 11. Court Holds / Travel Restrictions (background note, not confirmed on DoPassport site)

Some secondary legal guides mention that a pending court order, "lookout notice," or similar travel-restriction flag can delay or block passport issuance until it is lifted. This was **not found on the DoPassport site pages reviewed** for this file and should be treated as unverified background context only — do not present it to a user as an official DoPassport policy statement without further confirmation.
