# Nepal Passport — Application (Form) Guide

> Source of record: Department of Passports (DoPassport) official process pages — https://nepalpassport.gov.np/process/-4, /-6, /-2, /-3 and the DoPassport online pre-enrolment portal documentation (fetched September 2026).

## Overview: The 4-Stage Process (official)

Every ordinary e-passport application — new, renewal, lost, or damaged — follows the **same four stages**:

1. **Fill the online pre-enrolment form.**
2. **Register in person** at your chosen enrolment centre (verify the form → pay the fee → biometric capture).
3. **Wait for notification** (email/SMS) that the passport is ready.
4. **Collect the passport** in person from the same enrolment centre where you applied.

---

## Stage 1 — Online Pre-Enrolment Form

- Go to the official online form: **https://online.nepalpassport.gov.np** (linked from nepalpassport.gov.np as "ePassport को लागी आवेदन दिनुहोस्").
- You can fill it from home on a computer or mobile phone. A dedicated **mobile app** ("Nepal ePassport", on the App Store and Google Play) is also available for filling the form on mobile.
- **Fill it yourself, carefully** — DoPassport explicitly advises applicants to complete their own form accurately so details don't mismatch later.
- While filling the form you must choose:
  - Your **Enrolment Centre**: the District/Area Administration Office, the Department of Passports itself, or (if abroad) the relevant embassy/consulate.
  - An **Appointment date and time slot** that suits you.
- After submitting, you receive a **complete copy of the form with a barcode and QR code**. **Print this form** and bring it to your registration centre at the scheduled time.
  - If you can't print it, you may bring it saved on your mobile phone — but it must be the **original digital form/PDF, not a screenshot**.

### Data entry notes from the official pre-enrolment guide

- **National ID (NIN)** is mandatory for applicants applying from within Nepal (see eligibility rules — not required under age 16 or for embassy applications).
- Do **not** enter a hyphen (-) or slash (/) when typing your citizenship certificate number.
- If your **place of birth is outside Nepal**, select "Other (Countries)" in the Place of Birth field, then choose the correct country in the Birth Country field.
- **Documents to upload:**
  - **First-time (new) issuance:** citizenship certificate — front and back.
  - **Renewal / Lost / Damaged:** citizenship certificate (front & back) **and** old passport (front & back).
  - Keep uploaded file sizes within the portal's stated limits.
- **Review your Application Summary carefully** before submitting, and correct any mistakes at this stage.
- Complete the **CAPTCHA** correctly to finish submission.
- If you spot an error **after** submitting the pre-enrolment form, DoPassport has a dedicated FAQ process for corrections ("Pre-enrollment गरी अनलाइन आवेदन फाराम भरी Submit गरे पश्चात फाराममा त्रुटि भेटिएमा के गर्ने?") — contact your chosen enrolment centre before your appointment if possible.

---

## Stage 2 — In-Person Registration

At your chosen enrolment centre, on your appointment date, complete three steps in order:

1. **Form verification (फाराम रुजू गर्ने)** — an officer checks your printed/digital form and documents.
2. **Fee payment (राजस्व भुक्तानी गर्ने)** — pay the applicable fee (see `passport_basics.md` for the current official fee table). Payment channel (bank, online, cash) varies by centre — confirm with your specific enrolment centre in advance.
3. **Biometric registration (दर्ता गर्ने)** — your **photograph is taken live** and your **biometric details** (fingerprints, etc.) are captured at the counter.

### Documents to bring (adult, applying inside Nepal)

- Original **Nepali Citizenship Certificate**, plus a photocopy.
- Your **printed pre-enrolment form** (with barcode/QR code) — or the saved digital original on your phone if you couldn't print it.
- **National ID (NIN)** — required if you are 16 or older and applying inside Nepal.
- **Old passport** (original), if this is a renewal, or a replacement for a lost/damaged passport.
- Payment for the applicable fee.

*(For documents required by minors, applicants abroad, and special-category passports, see `passport_special_cases.md`.)*

---

## Stage 3 — Notification

- Once your passport is ready, DoPassport notifies you by **email or SMS**.
- Wait for this notification before travelling to collect your passport — don't assume a fixed date without confirmation, since processing time varies by centre (see `passport_basics.md` §8).

---

## Stage 4 — Collection

- Collect your passport **in person, from the same location where you applied** (Department of Passports, the DAO/Area Administration Office, or the embassy/consulate abroad).
- **Embassies/consulates abroad** may offer delivery by post, or require in-person collection — this depends on the specific mission's arrangement, so check with the mission that processed your application.
- Bring the necessary identifying documents that your enrolment centre specifies when confirming your passport is ready.

---

## Applies to New, Renewal, and All Other Cases

DoPassport is explicit that **the same 4-stage process applies whether you are getting a brand-new passport, renewing, or replacing a lost/damaged one** — only the specific documents and the fee amount differ by case (see `passport_special_cases.md`).

---

## Practical Tips for a Smooth Application (for chatbot responses)

- Double-check that every field matches your **citizenship certificate exactly** (name, date of birth, parents' names) — mismatches are a common cause of delay or rejection.
- Bring **both originals and photocopies** of every required document.
- Don't wear glare-producing jewellery, heavy makeup, or tika for your photo; a **permanent** tattoo/mark at or above the neck is not a problem, but a **temporary** one may be worth letting heal first (see `passport_faq.md`).
- Keep your printed form and appointment confirmation safe — you need the **application number/barcode** at every subsequent step.
- If you must **cancel or reschedule** your appointment slot after booking, DoPassport has a dedicated process for this ("मैले फारम भरेर एउटा समय लिई सके उक्त समय क्यान्सिल (cancel) अथवा परिवर्तन गर्न के गर्नुपर्ला") — contact your chosen enrolment centre.
- If no appointment slots appear available at an Area Office or a Nepali mission abroad, this is a known/tracked FAQ topic ("इलाका कार्यालय तथा नेपाल स्थित दुताबासमा एपोइन्टमेन्ट स्लट देखिँदैन के कारण होला") — it usually reflects centre capacity; keep checking the portal, or contact the centre directly.
