# Nepal Passport — Frequently Asked Questions

> Source of record: Department of Passports (DoPassport) official FAQ section — https://nepalpassport.gov.np/process/process-2 and linked pages (fetched September 2026). Each answer below reflects the official page content unless marked "(secondary source)".

---

**Q: How long does it take to get a passport?**
A: It depends on where you apply. Department of Passports (Tripureshwar, Kathmandu): within 2 working days. District/Area Administration Office: 15–45 days. Nepali embassy/consulate abroad: 15 days to 2 months. Contact the specific enrolment centre (info@nepalpassport.gov.np) for an exact estimate.

**Q: My passport got lost — what should I do?**
A: To prevent misuse, immediately apply at the nearest enrolment centre, with your identification, to have the lost passport **cancelled**. To get a replacement, you must submit a completely fresh application and go through the entire new-passport process again (same steps, applicable "lost/damaged" fee).

**Q: I found my old passport after already getting a new one to replace the "lost" one — what now?**
A: DoPassport has a specific process for this situation (see the linked FAQ "हराएको राहदानीको सट्टा नयाँ राहदानी बनाइसकेपछि पुरानो राहदानी भेटियो, के गर्ने?" on the official FAQ index) — contact your enrolment centre; the recovered passport cannot simply be used again since it was already cancelled/reported lost.

**Q: Can I wear jewellery in my passport photo?**
A: Everyday, small jewellery (e.g., a small nose stud you always wear) is fine, **provided it does not create glare/shine in the photo and does not cover key facial features** (eyes, mouth, eyebrows, etc.). Large or "occasion" jewellery (e.g., party jewellery) must be removed before the photo is taken.

**Q: Can I wear makeup or tika (religious mark) in my passport photo?**
A: It's best to avoid makeup where possible. Regular, everyday makeup that doesn't change your facial appearance is acceptable. There's no strict written rule about tika specifically, but it is advisable to **not wear tika** when the photo is taken.

**Q: I have a permanent tattoo or mark on my neck or face — is that a problem?**
A: A **permanent** tattoo or mark on the neck or above does not need to be addressed — you can proceed with your application as normal. If the mark is **temporary** (e.g., from an injury) and you need the passport urgently, you may proceed now and apply for a fresh passport once the mark has healed; otherwise it's advisable to wait until the mark clears, since it can cause difficulty during travel/border checks.

**Q: Is a photograph required in hand when applying for the e-passport?**
A: The e-passport process captures a **live photo** at the enrolment counter as part of biometric registration — check the specific enrolment centre's current instructions, as requirements can vary slightly by centre (see DoPassport FAQ "विद्युतीय राहदानी (ePassport) का लागि राहदानी आवेदकले फोटो साथमा ल्याउनु पर्छ कि पर्दैन?").

**Q: Is the National ID (NID/NIN) mandatory for a passport application?**
A: **Yes, for applicants aged 16 and above applying from within Nepal.** It is **not mandatory** for applicants under 16, or for applications submitted at a Nepali embassy/consulate abroad.

**Q: Should I apply for the 34-page or 66-page passport? Can anyone apply for the 66-page one?**
A: Any applicant may choose either size. Choose the **66-page** booklet if you travel frequently and expect the standard 34 pages to run out; otherwise the **34-page** booklet is sufficient for most people.

**Q: My Machine-Readable Passport (MRP) hasn't expired yet — do I still need to get an e-passport?**
A: **No.** As long as your MRP remains within its validity period, you can keep using it to travel abroad. You should apply for a new e-passport if: less than one year of validity remains, the passport is lost/torn/burnt/otherwise damaged, it has an error that makes it unusable, or its pages are full.

**Q: When should I renew my passport?**
A: DoPassport addresses renewal timing directly on its FAQ index (see "राहदानी नवीकरण कहिले गर्ने?"); as a rule of thumb, apply once validity is running low (see the MRP guidance above — under one year of remaining validity is when a fresh/renewed passport becomes advisable) or immediately if the passport is damaged, full, or contains an error.

**Q: Does renewing keep my old passport number? What happens to visas already stamped in the old passport?**
A: DoPassport has dedicated FAQ entries on this ("के राहदानी नवीकरण गर्दा पुरानो सङ्ख्या (Number) कै राहदानी बन्दछ? मेरो राहदानीमा भिसा छ, नवीकरण गर्दा के हुन्छ?"). If you need to preserve a valid foreign visa in your old (now-renewed) booklet, raise this at the counter — enrolment centres commonly cancel the old book (corner-cut) and return it alongside the new passport so valid visas remain evidence for travel; confirm the exact handling with your enrolment centre.

**Q: I applied for a passport at a Nepali mission abroad, but had to return to Nepal before it was ready — what do I do?**
A: This exact scenario has a dedicated official FAQ entry ("विदेशस्थित नियोगमा राहदानीको लागि आवेदन दिएकोमा राहदानी प्राप्त नुहुँदै नेपाल फर्कनुपर्‍यो, के गर्ने?") — contact the issuing mission and DoPassport (info@nepalpassport.gov.np) directly, as the resolution depends on where the booklet is in production.

**Q: Can a relative in Nepal collect/rush a passport for me while I'm urgently abroad?**
A: **No.** If you urgently need a passport while abroad, apply for a **temporary (handwritten) passport** at the relevant Nepali mission, or travel to Nepal using a **One Way Travel Document** if you have no valid passport at all.

**Q: What is a "temporary passport"?**
A: A **temporary passport** is a handwritten travel document that a Nepali mission abroad can issue when you've applied for an e-passport but urgently need travel documentation before it's ready. It generally **cannot be used for normal international travel**, though in some countries it can be used to apply for a visa, and it can be used to return to Nepal. Whether a given country accepts a handwritten temporary passport for a visa application should be confirmed with that country's authorities before relying on it.

**Q: If there's an error in my printed passport, how do I fix it?**
A: If the passport was printed with incorrect details, you must obtain a fresh passport — this means completing the **entire application process again** and paying the applicable fee; there is no simple "correction" shortcut once the booklet is printed.

**Q: Can I change the "person to notify" (contact person) listed on my passport?**
A: Yes — DoPassport has a specific FAQ entry on this ("के राहदानीमा खबर गर्ने व्यक्तिको नाम बदल्न सकिन्छ?"); contact your enrolment centre for the exact update procedure.

**Q: Can I apply from a district other than where my citizenship was issued?**
A: DoPassport's FAQ index directly addresses this scenario ("हाल म नागरिकता लिएको भन्दा अर्कै जिल्लामा बस्ने गर्छु के त्यहाँबाट राहदानीको लागि आवेदन दिन सकिन्छ") — applying from your current district of residence, rather than your citizenship-issuing district, is generally accommodated; confirm document requirements with the local enrolment centre.

**Q: I don't have a copy of my lost passport at all — how do I still apply for a new one?**
A: This exact situation has its own official FAQ entry ("मेरो राहदानी हरायो, मसँग प्रतिलिपि पनि छैन। नयाँ राहदानी आवेदन दिन यसको प्रतिलिपी कसरी पाउन सकिएला?" / "हराएको वा झुत्रो राहदानीको अभावमा राहदानी आवेदन प्रक्रिया") — you can still start a fresh application; DoPassport can generally retrieve your prior record from their own system. Bring your citizenship certificate and any other identifying documents to the enrolment centre and explain the situation.

**Q: Does the 66-page passport get visas approved more easily than the 34-page one?**
A: DoPassport's FAQ index directly addresses (and by implication dispels) this belief ("के ६६ पेजको राहदानीमा भिसा लाग्ने सम्भावना बढी हुन्छ") — booklet size is about page capacity for stamps/visas, not a factor that itself improves visa approval odds. Visa decisions rest with the destination country.

**Q: How do I pay the passport fee?**
A: Payment method (online, bank transfer, or cash) **varies by enrolment centre** — check with your specific registration centre (DoPassport, DAO, or embassy/consulate) for the accepted payment channel there.

**Q: Where can I get more information or help?**
A: Contact the enrolment centre where you applied (or intend to apply) directly. General DoPassport contact: info@nepalpassport.gov.np, hotline +977-1-5970330; district queries: district@nepalpassport.gov.np.

**Q: What documents are required for an Official/Service or Diplomatic passport?**
A: Requirements are set by the relevant ministry, constitutional body, or office you serve under — DoPassport directs applicants to that sponsoring authority for the exact checklist, and also publishes a downloadable document list ("Documents required for Official, Service and Diplomatic Passport") from its official file server.

**Q: How do I check my passport application status?**
A: Use the "राहदानीको स्थिति जाँच गर्नुहोस्" (Check Passport Status) tool on the DoPassport homepage (nepalpassport.gov.np) — you'll need your citizenship number, date of birth (AD), place of birth, and surname to look it up.
