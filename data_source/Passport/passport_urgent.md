Fastest way to get a Nepali passport (urgent / fast-track):

1\. Apply at the Department of Passports, Tripureshwor, Kathmandu — this is the ONLY office that offers urgent/fast-track service.  
2\. Fill the online form at https://emrtds.nepalpassport.gov.np/ — select the appropriate application type.  
3\. Processing time: 2 working days.  
4\. Additional fast-track surcharge: \+NPR 7,000 (34-page) or \+NPR 12,000 (66-page) on top of the standard fee.  
5\. Bring all required original documents to your appointment.  
6\. Regular DAO/AAO offices do NOT offer fast-track — only the Department of Passports does.


Fast-track vs normal passport process in Nepal:
FAST-TRACK (urgent):  
\- Where: Department of Passports, Tripureshwor, Kathmandu only  
\- Processing time: 2 working days  
\- Extra cost: \+NPR 7,000 (34-page) or \+NPR 12,000 (66-page) surcharge  
\- Who: any applicant from any district  
\- Steps: same online form → same biometric enrollment → faster processing

NORMAL (regular):  
\- Where: District/Area Administration Office (DAO/AAO) in your district  
\- Processing time: 15–45 days  
\- Extra cost: none (standard fee only)  
\- Who: residents of that district (or with Migration Certificate)  
\- Steps: same online form → same biometric enrollment → standard processing

Both require: in-person biometric enrollment, same documents, same online application form.
