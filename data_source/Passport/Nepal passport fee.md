Nepal passport fee — 34-page vs 66-page:

New or Renewal:  
\- Adult (16+): 34-page \= NPR 12,000 | 66-page \= NPR 20,000  
\- Minor (under 10): 34-page \= NPR 9,500 | 66-page \= NPR 14,500

Lost / Damaged / Unusable:  
\- Adult: 34-page \= NPR 17,000 | 66-page \= NPR 25,000  
\- Minor (under 10): 34-page \= NPR 14,500 | 66-page \= NPR 19,500

Office error: Free of charge (no fee).  
Fast-track surcharge: \+NPR 7,000 (34-page), \+NPR 12,000 (66-page).  
Fees at Nepali missions abroad may differ — contact the relevant mission.  

Fee for replacing a lost or damaged Nepali passport:
- Adult 34-page: NPR 17,000
- Adult 66-page: NPR 25,000
- Minor (under 10) 34-page: NPR 14,500
- Minor (under 10) 66-page: NPR 19,500

No police report or newspaper notice is required. The Department of Passports notifies Interpol directly. Select "Replacement – Lost/Stolen" as the application type.
