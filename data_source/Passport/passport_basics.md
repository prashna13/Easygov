# Nepal Passport — Basics

> Source of record: Department of Passports (DoPassport), Government of Nepal — https://nepalpassport.gov.np (official portal, fee/process pages fetched September 2026). Where a fact could not be confirmed on the DoPassport site itself, the source is marked explicitly.

## 1. Issuing Authority

- **Department of Passports (राहदानी विभाग / DoPassport)**, under the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA)**, is the sole authority that issues Nepali passports.
- Head office: **Tripureshwar, Kathmandu, Nepal.**
- Contact: Hotline +977-1-5970330 / +977-1-5970329, email info@nepalpassport.gov.np, communication@nepalpassport.gov.np, dg@nepalpassport.gov.np. District branch: district@nepalpassport.gov.np.
- Official working hours: **Sunday–Friday... officially listed as Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM** on the portal.
- Official web portal: https://nepalpassport.gov.np — hosts the pre-enrolment system (https://online.nepalpassport.gov.np), fee schedule, status-check tool, notices, and downloadable forms.
- Official mobile app: "Nepal ePassport" — available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (published by DoPassport).
- Related official bodies linked from the DoPassport site: Ministry of Foreign Affairs (mofa.gov.np), Ministry of Home Affairs (moha.gov.np), Department of Consular Services (nepalconsular.gov.np), Department of Immigration (immigration.gov.np), Department of Foreign Employment (dofe.gov.np), National ID Card and Civil Registration Department (donidcr.gov.np).

## 2. What the Nepali Passport Is

- Nepal has issued a **biometric e-passport** since it replaced the older **Machine-Readable Passport (MRP)** on 17 November 2021.
- The e-passport is a polycarbonate booklet with an embedded contactless chip compliant with **ICAO Doc 9303**, storing the holder's photo, fingerprints, and personal data. *(Detail on chip/ICAO/IDEMIA sourced from secondary legal-guide summaries, not confirmed verbatim on the DoPassport site — treat as background context, not an official quote.)*
- Legal basis: **Passport Act, 2076 (2019)** and **Passport Rules, 2077 (2020)**, published by the Nepal Law Commission (lawcommission.gov.np). The Act recognises three passport categories (Section 3): **diplomatic, official/service, and ordinary**.

## 3. Types of Passport

| Type | Who it's for | Where to learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary passport (साधारण राहदानी)** | Any Nepali citizen — the type this guide mainly covers | nepalpassport.gov.np |
| **Official / Service passport** | Government employees and officials travelling on deputation | Apply through the relevant ministry/constitutional body; document checklist: DoPassport's official download (see passport_special_cases.md) |
| **Diplomatic passport** | Diplomats and officials nominated by the Government of Nepal | Apply through MoFA / the nominating ministry |

## 4. Booklet Sizes

- **34-page booklet** — standard choice for most applicants.
- **66-page booklet** — meant for frequent travellers who fill pages quickly.
- Officially: *"If you travel frequently and feel 34 pages may not be enough, apply for the 66-page passport; otherwise the 34-page passport is sufficient."* Both are open to any applicant — there is no eligibility restriction on choosing the 66-page option.

## 5. Validity

- **Adults (18+):** 10 years.
- **Minors (below 18):** 5 years (per secondary legal-guide sources; not independently re-confirmed word-for-word on the DoPassport FAQ pages reviewed, but consistent with the age-tiered fee table DoPassport itself publishes — see below).

## 6. Eligibility

- Any Nepali citizen holding a valid **Nepali Citizenship Certificate (Nagarikta)** is eligible for an ordinary passport.
- **Nepal does not recognise dual citizenship** under the Constitution of Nepal 2072 — a person who has acquired foreign nationality is not eligible for a Nepali passport. *(Sourced from secondary legal summaries; consistent with the Constitution's known citizenship provisions.)*
- **National ID (राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र, NID/NIN):** Mandatory for applicants **aged 16 and above who are applying from within Nepal**. It is **not required** for applicants under 16, or for anyone applying through a Nepali diplomatic mission abroad. *(Confirmed directly on nepalpassport.gov.np.)*
- Minors and applicants without a citizenship certificate (e.g., dependents abroad) follow special procedures — see `passport_special_cases.md`.

## 7. Where You Can Apply

- **Department of Passports, Tripureshwar, Kathmandu** — the central/head office.
- **District Administration Office (DAO) or Area Administration Office** in any of Nepal's 77 districts — all now accept biometric enrolment.
- **Nepali Embassy or Consulate-General** — for Nepali citizens residing abroad.

## 8. Official Processing Times

*(Confirmed directly on nepalpassport.gov.np — these vary by enrolment centre, not by a "regular vs. fast-track" fee tier as some third-party sites claim.)*

| Enrolment centre | Typical processing time |
|---|---|
| Department of Passports (Tripureshwar, Kathmandu) | Within **2 working days** |
| District/Area Administration Office | **15 to 45 days** |
| Nepali Embassy/Consulate abroad | **15 days to 2 months** |

For exact turnaround, DoPassport directs applicants to contact the specific enrolment centre (info@nepalpassport.gov.np).

## 9. Official Fee Schedule (current, from nepalpassport.gov.np/process/-41)

| No. | Category | 34-page fee | 66-page fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New / Renewal (age 10 and above) | NPR 12,000 | NPR 20,000 |
| 2 | New / Renewal (minor under 10 years) | NPR 9,500 | NPR 14,500 |
| 3 | Lost, or booklet unusable due to torn pages, casing damage, or other damage | NPR 17,000 | NPR 25,000 |
| 4 | Lost / Damaged (minor under 10 years) | NPR 14,500 | NPR 19,500 |
| 5 | Official Error (DoPassport's own mistake) | Free | Free |

**Important note on fees:** Several third-party guides (blogs, notary/legal-service sites) advertise a two-tier "regular / fast-track" fee schedule (e.g., NPR 5,000 regular vs. NPR 10,000 fast-track for a 34-page passport). That schedule appears to reflect an **older/superseded fee notice**. The table above was read directly from the DoPassport website's live fee page and should be treated as the authoritative current figure. Always tell users to double-check the live fee page (nepalpassport.gov.np → "राहदानीका लागि लाग्ने दस्तुर") or the enrolment centre before paying, since MoFA/DoPassport can revise fees by notice.

## 10. MRP (old-style) Passport Holders

- If your **Machine-Readable Passport (MRP)** is still within its validity, you do **not** need to switch to an e-passport — you can keep travelling on it. *(Confirmed on nepalpassport.gov.np.)*
- You should apply for a new (e-)passport if: the MRP has **less than one year of validity remaining**, or it is **lost, torn, burnt, otherwise damaged**, has an **error** making it unusable, or its **pages are full**.

## 11. Related Filing/Document Notes for a Chatbot

- Users often confuse the **passport** with the **National ID (NIN)** or the **Citizenship Certificate** — the passport application depends on both but is a separate document/process from each.
- For citizenship-specific queries, defer to the citizenship knowledge base files (`citizenship_basics.md`, etc.).
