# Business / Company Registration in Nepal — Basics

---

## Quick Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official Name** | Company Registration (Kambani Darta) |
| **Governing Law** | Companies Act 2063 (2006) · Companies Regulations 2064 · Industrial Enterprises Act 2076 · FITTA 2019 (for foreign investment) |
| **Issuing Authority** | Office of Company Registrar (OCR) under Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies |
| **Online Portal** | camis.ocr.gov.np (CAMIS — Company Administration and Management Information System) |
| **Company Check Portal** | ocr.gov.np |
| **Most Common Type** | Private Limited Company (Pvt. Ltd.) |
| **Minimum Shareholders** | 1 (Private Ltd.) · 7 (Public Ltd.) |
| **Minimum Paid-Up Capital** | NPR 1,00,000 (1 Lakh) for Private Ltd. |
| **Govt. Fee Range** | NPR 1,000 – NPR 43,000+ (based on authorized capital) |
| **Processing Time** | 7–10 working days (complete documents) · Up to 21 days with queries |
| **Office Location** | Tripureshwor, Kathmandu (Head Office) + Provincial Offices |
| **Office Hours** | Sunday to Friday · 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM |

## Why Register a Company?

A registered company is a separate legal entity. This means:

- Can own property, open bank accounts, enter contracts, and sue or be sued in its own name
- Shareholders' personal assets are protected — liability is limited to their investment
- Required to receive bank loans, government contracts, and investment
- Access to government subsidies, tax benefits, and startup support programs
- Protection of your business name and brand identity
- Mandatory for legal business operations under Nepal law

> Operating as an unregistered business is illegal under the Companies Act 2063 and can lead to fines and legal restrictions.

## Types of Companies in Nepal

| Type | Min. Shareholders | Max. Shareholders | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Private Limited Company** | 1 | 101 | Most common · Cannot raise funds from public · Limited liability · Ideal for startups and SMEs |
| **Public Limited Company** | 7 | Unlimited | Can issue shares to public · Can list on stock exchange · More compliance obligations |
| **Profit Not-Distributing Company** | 1 | Unlimited | For NGOs, academic, welfare, or social purposes · No dividends to shareholders |
| **Foreign Branch Office** | — | — | Foreign company conducting commercial activities in Nepal · Must register with OCR + comply with FITTA |
| **Foreign Liaison Office** | — | — | Non-commercial activities only (research, coordination) · No revenue-generating activities allowed |
| **Single Shareholder Company** | 1 (individual) | 1 | One person company · Same person can be sole director |
| **Holding / Subsidiary Company** | — | — | Parent-subsidiary corporate structure |

> **For most startups, small businesses, and entrepreneurs: register a Private Limited Company.** It is the fastest, cheapest, and simplest structure.

## Required Documents

### Private Limited Company (Standard)

| Document | Details |
|---|---|
| Application Form (Anusuchi 1) | As per OCR format — with NPR 5 revenue stamp |
| Memorandum of Association (MOA / Prabandha Patra) | Two signed copies — defines company name, objectives, capital, liability |
| Articles of Association (AOA / Niyamawali) | Two signed copies — defines internal governance rules |
| Citizenship Certificate | Attested copies of all promoters / shareholders |
| Registered Office Proof | Rental agreement or ownership document for the office address |
| Passport-size Photos | Of all promoters / directors |
| Mutual Agreement | Among shareholders (if multiple promoters) |
| Director Consent Letters | Written consent of each director to act as director |

### Additional for Public Limited Companies
- Prospectus (if raising funds from public)
- Board resolution
- Additional shareholder documentation

### For Foreign Investors (FDI)
- Foreign investment approval from Department of Industry (DOI) or Investment Board Nepal (IBN)
- Passport copies of foreign shareholders / directors (notarized and apostilled in the home country)
- Visa documentation
- DOI approval letter
- Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) approval for capital repatriation
- Minimum investment: NPR 2,00,00,000 (2 Crore / 20 Million) per foreign investor

## Application Process — Step by Step

**Step 1 — Create Account on CAMIS Portal**
- Visit: camis.ocr.gov.np/login
- Register with your name, email address, mobile number, and other details
- Verify your account via email confirmation
- This account is used for all steps: name reservation, application, document upload, payment, and certificate download

**Step 2 — Name Reservation**
- Log in to CAMIS and search for name availability in both English and Nepali
- Submit up to 3 proposed names (ranked by preference)
- Names are checked for: similarity with existing companies, restricted words, compliance with naming rules
- Approval or rejection via email within 1–3 working days
- Once approved: you have 90 days to complete the full registration

**Step 3 — Prepare MOA and AOA**
- Memorandum of Association (Prabandha Patra): defines company name, registered office, business objectives, authorized capital, liability type
- Articles of Association (Niyamawali): defines internal rules — board meetings, shareholder rights, voting procedures, dividend policy
- Prepare two signed copies of each
- Both are accepted in Nepali or English; most consultants draft in Nepali for OCR submission
- Recommended: engage a legal consultant or company secretary for drafting

**Step 4 — Online Application Submission**
- Log in to CAMIS and fill the company registration application
- Enter: shareholders details, directors details, registered office address, authorized capital, number of shares, face value per share
- Upload scanned copies of all required documents (PDF or JPEG format, within prescribed file size)
- Review all details carefully and submit

**Step 5 — OCR Review and Query Resolution**
- OCR officers examine submitted documents for completeness and compliance with Companies Act 2063
- If discrepancies are found: OCR issues a query notice via CAMIS dashboard
- Respond to queries within 7 days to avoid application rejection
- Correction and resubmission through CAMIS

**Step 6 — Fee Payment**
- Upon OCR approval of documents, a payment notice is generated on CAMIS
- Pay via: e-banking, mobile banking (eSewa, Khalti), debit/credit cards through the CAMIS payment gateway
- Fees under NPR 5,000: pay online through portal
- Fees above NPR 5,000: bank deposit and upload voucher
- Keep the payment receipt

**Step 7 — Certificate of Incorporation**
- Upon successful payment and final OCR verification, the Certificate of Incorporation is issued digitally through CAMIS
- Download and save the certificate — this is your company's official legal identity document

**Step 8 — Post-Registration Compliance (Mandatory)**
See the Post-Registration section below — several steps are mandatory immediately after receiving the certificate.

## Post-Registration Compliance (Mandatory Steps)

After receiving the Certificate of Incorporation, the following are mandatory:

| Task | Deadline | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| PAN Registration | Within 7 days | Inland Revenue Department (IRD) |
| Ward Office Business Registration | Within 30 days | Local Municipality / Ward Office |
| First Board Meeting | Within 30 days | Internal |
| Share Lagat Filing (Shareholder Details) | Within 90 days | OCR |
| Issue Share Certificates | Within 60 days | Internal |
| Open Company Bank Account | As soon as possible | Any commercial bank |
| Register Company Seal | After incorporation | Internal |
| VAT Registration (if applicable) | Before first transaction if turnover threshold exceeded | IRD |
| Auditor Appointment | Before first AGM | Internal — notify OCR within 15 days |
| Social Security Fund (SSF) Registration | Within 30 days of hiring employees | Social Security Fund |
| Annual General Meeting (AGM) | Within 6 months of fiscal year end | Internal |
| Annual Return Filing at OCR | Within 60 days of fiscal year end | OCR |
| Sector-specific licenses | As required by business type | Relevant regulatory authority |

> **PAN is mandatory before any financial transaction.** Without PAN, you cannot open a bank account, raise an invoice, hire employees on payroll, or file taxes.

## Verifying a Registered Company

To check whether a company is legally registered with the OCR:
1. Visit: ocr.gov.np
2. Search company by name or registration number
3. Results show: registration status, registered office, directors, and other public details
4. Always verify before entering a business agreement, partnership, or investment

## Important Notes

- The entire registration process is digital through CAMIS — physical visits are not required for most steps, but some offices may require original document submission for verification
- You cannot use a PO Box as a registered office address — a physical address with proof (rental agreement or ownership document) is mandatory
- Company name on MOA must exactly match the OCR-approved name — any mismatch causes rejection
- Respond to OCR queries within 7 days — failure to respond results in application rejection
- The Share Lagat (shareholder details) must be filed within 90 days of registration — late filing results in compounding penalties
- OCR provincial offices are available for businesses outside Kathmandu — you do not need to come to Kathmandu to register
