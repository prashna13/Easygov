# Business Registration Special Cases — Nepal

---

## Overview

Certain business types, ownership structures, or situations require additional steps or a different process. Use the sections below to find your specific case.

---

## Special Case Quick Reference

| Situation | What's Different |
|---|---|
| Single-person company | Minimum 1 shareholder and 1 director — same person can hold both roles |
| Foreign investor (FDI) | DOI / IBN approval required first; minimum NPR 2 Crore investment |
| Foreign branch office | Commercial activities in Nepal — register with OCR + comply with FITTA |
| Foreign liaison office | Non-commercial only — no revenue allowed |
| Public limited company | Minimum 7 promoters; additional compliance obligations |
| Non-profit company | No dividends; social/welfare/academic purposes only |
| Regulated sector (banking, insurance, etc.) | Sector-specific license required before operations |
| NRN (Non-Resident Nepali) registering a company | Can register remotely via Power of Attorney chain through CAMIS |
| Name rejected by OCR | Submit alternative names or revise to comply with naming rules |
| OCR query raised | Respond within 7 days through CAMIS dashboard |

---

## Single-Person / Single-Shareholder Company

The Companies Act 2063 allows one person to register a Private Limited Company.

- Minimum: 1 shareholder and 1 director (same person can hold both positions)
- No partner or co-founder required
- All standard documents apply: MOA, AOA, citizenship copy, registered office proof
- Standard fees and process apply

---

## Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) — Company Registration

Foreign nationals can register companies in Nepal but must follow additional steps before the standard OCR process.

### Minimum Investment Requirement
- NPR 2,00,00,000 (2 Crore / NPR 20 Million) per foreign investor
- This is the minimum threshold for FDI approval

### Step-by-Step FDI Registration Process

**Part A — Before OCR Registration:**
1. Obtain prior approval from Department of Industry (DOI) or Investment Board Nepal (IBN) — depending on investment size
2. Submit investment proposal with: business objectives, capital structure, technology transfer details, projected employment
3. Receive DOI/IBN approval letter
4. Record the investment with Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) for capital repatriation rights

**Part B — Standard OCR Registration (same as local company):**
1. Create CAMIS account
2. Reserve company name
3. Prepare and submit MOA, AOA, and documents
4. Include DOI approval letter as an additional required document
5. Foreign shareholders must provide notarized and apostilled passport copies from their home country
6. Pay registration fee and receive Certificate of Incorporation

**Part C — Post-Registration (FDI Specific):**
- File investment details with NRB within prescribed period
- Obtain any sector-specific licenses
- Comply with FITTA 2019 for profit repatriation

**Timeline for FDI Companies:** 4–8 weeks additional compared to domestic registration

## Foreign Branch Office

A foreign company conducting commercial activities within Nepal must register a branch office.

- Legal basis: Section 2(f) of Companies Act 2063 + FITTA 2019
- Must register with OCR
- Must have a local representative in Nepal
- Complies with Nepalese taxation and labor laws
- OCR registration fee: NPR 5,000–25,000 depending on investment amount + annual renewal fees
- Cannot operate as a liaison (non-commercial) office — branch offices must actually conduct commercial activity

**Required documents for branch office:**
- Parent company's certificate of incorporation (from home country)
- MOA/AOA of parent company
- Board resolution authorizing Nepal branch
- Power of attorney for local representative
- Local representative's citizenship / passport
- Registered office address proof in Nepal
- All foreign documents must be notarized and apostilled

---

## Foreign Liaison / Representative Office

For non-commercial activities only (research, coordination, market study).

- No revenue-generating activities permitted
- Must register with OCR
- Lower fee structure than branch office
- Must renew registration annually
- If any commercial activity begins, must convert to branch office or new company

---

## Non-Profit / Profit-Not-Distributing Company

For organizations established for social, academic, charitable, or welfare purposes.

- No dividends distributed to shareholders or members
- Can generate revenue — must reinvest in stated objectives
- Same registration process through CAMIS
- MOA must clearly state non-profit objectives
- Must comply with annual reporting and audit requirements same as profit companies

> NGOs and non-profit organizations have a separate registration process through the Social Welfare Council (SWC) and District Administration Office — this is different from OCR registration. Consult the relevant authority for the structure that fits your purpose.

## NRN (Non-Resident Nepali) Registering a Company Remotely

NRNs abroad can register a company in Nepal without being physically present.

**Process:**
1. Engage a legal representative or law firm in Nepal
2. Execute a Power of Attorney (PoA) authorizing the representative to act on your behalf
3. PoA must be notarized in the country of residence and apostilled (or embassy-attested for non-Apostille countries)
4. Forward original PoA and notarized citizenship/passport copies to your representative in Nepal
5. Representative completes the CAMIS process and signs documents on your behalf
6. Certificate of Incorporation is issued in the company's name

**Note:** For NRN citizenship holders, check NRN-specific investment provisions under the NRN Act 2064 (2008) which may offer additional rights compared to a standard foreign investor.

## Public Limited Company

For larger businesses intending to raise capital from the public or list on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE).

**Key differences from Private Limited:**
- Minimum 7 promoters required
- Can issue shares to the general public
- Can list on NEPSE (requires Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON) approval separately)
- Higher compliance obligations: stricter audit requirements, quarterly financial disclosures, mandatory AGM
- More complex MOA and AOA requirements
- Government fee structure differs from private companies

## Company Name Reservation — Rules and Rejection Reasons

### Name Rules Under Companies Act 2063
- Must be unique — not similar to any existing registered company
- Must be submitted in both English and Nepali
- Cannot contain restricted words without separate approval: Nepal, Bank, University, Government, National, Royal, Insurance, etc.
- Cannot be too generic (e.g., "Nepal Trading Company")
- Cannot imply government ownership or patronage
- Cannot be misleading about the company's activities

### What to Do If Your Name Is Rejected
1. Review the rejection reason on your CAMIS dashboard
2. Prepare 2–3 alternative names before submitting
3. Make names more specific and unique (add a coined word, location, or distinct element)
4. Resubmit through CAMIS — no additional fee for re-submission of a name
5. Name approval takes 1–3 working days per attempt

## Handling OCR Queries

If OCR raises a query on your submitted application:

1. Log in to CAMIS dashboard — query details are shown under your application
2. Review the specific issue flagged (document mismatch, missing field, formatting error, etc.)
3. Correct the relevant document or information
4. Re-upload corrected files through CAMIS
5. Resubmit within 7 days of query notice
6. Applications not responded to within 7 days may be rejected — you would need to restart

**Most common query reasons:**
- MOA objectives too vague or not matching selected company type
- Company name on MOA does not exactly match the OCR-approved name
- Citizenship copies not clearly legible
- Registered office proof does not match the address entered in the form
- Share capital structure inconsistency between MOA and the application form

## Capital Structure — What to Decide Before Applying

### Authorized Capital vs Paid-Up Capital

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Authorized Capital | Maximum total capital the company is legally allowed to have — stated in MOA |
| Paid-Up Capital | Amount actually invested by shareholders at time of registration |
| Face Value Per Share | NPR 100 or NPR 1,000 are typical in Nepal |

- Government fee is calculated on authorized capital — choose it realistically (you cannot reduce it later without a formal amendment, but can increase it)
- Minimum paid-up capital for Private Ltd.: NPR 1,00,000 (1 Lakh)
- Paid-up capital must be at least partially subscribed at registration

## Regulated Sectors — Special Approval Required Before Registration

| Sector | Authority | Key Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Banking and Financial Institutions | Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) | Minimum capital NPR 2 Billion |
| Insurance | Nepal Insurance Authority (NIA) | Separate licensing under Insurance Act 2079 |
| Hydropower / Energy | Ministry of Energy | Survey and generation license |
| Tourism / Trekking | Department of Tourism | Tourism license |
| Media / Broadcasting | Department of Information and Broadcasting | Broadcasting license |
| Manufacturing / Industry | Department of Industry | Industry registration |
| Hospitals / Clinics | Department of Health Services | Health facility license |
| Education Institutions | Ministry of Education | Affiliation or recognition |
| Food and Beverages | DFTQC | Food license / registration |

> For regulated sectors: obtain sector approval FIRST, then register at OCR. Operating without the required sectoral license is illegal regardless of OCR registration status.

## Amending a Registered Company

After registration, the following changes require formal OCR notification or approval:

| Change | Process | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Director appointment or resignation | Notify OCR through CAMIS | Within 15 days |
| Change of registered office address | Submit amendment through CAMIS | Within prescribed period |
| Increase in authorized capital | File amendment with OCR + pay additional fee | Before issuing new shares |
| Change of company name | Apply for new name reservation + amendment | Standard name approval process |
| Change of business objectives | File MOA amendment through CAMIS | Before commencing new activity |
| Share transfer | File with OCR (for private companies, shareholder approval required) | As per shareholders' agreement |

## Closing / De-registering a Company

If you want to close the company:

- Apply for voluntary dissolution or strike-off through CAMIS under the Directive Relating to Special Deregistration 2025
- File pending annual returns and clear outstanding dues with OCR and IRD
- Obtain a No Objection Letter from IRD confirming all tax liabilities are cleared
- Submit dissolution application through CAMIS
- OCR reviews and issues strike-off / dissolution notice
- Company is officially de-registered

> Do not simply stop operating — non-operational companies still accumulate penalties for missed annual returns. Use the formal de-registration process.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---|---|
| Name on MOA does not match OCR-approved name | Copy the approved name exactly from your CAMIS dashboard — even minor spelling differences cause rejection |
| Uploading blurry or incomplete document scans | Use a flatbed scanner or a document scanning app — every text must be clearly readable |
| Authorised capital too low for actual needs | Set realistic authorized capital upfront — increasing it later requires a formal amendment with additional fees |
| Forgetting to file Share Lagat within 90 days | Set a reminder immediately after receiving the certificate — penalties compound |
| Not registering for PAN before first transaction | PAN is mandatory before any invoice, bank transaction, or payroll — register within 7 days |
| Using home address without landlord consent | Get written consent from the landlord (or parent if living at family home) before listing as registered office |
| Not responding to OCR query within 7 days | Monitor your CAMIS dashboard regularly after submission — set email notifications |
