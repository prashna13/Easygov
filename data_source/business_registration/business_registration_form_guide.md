# Business Registration Form Guide — Nepal

---

## Overview

Company registration in Nepal is completed entirely online through the CAMIS portal at **camis.ocr.gov.np**. There is no physical paper form to fill manually — all fields are entered on screen, documents are uploaded, and the Certificate of Incorporation is issued digitally.

**Before you start, prepare:**
- Citizenship certificate of all promoters/shareholders (scanned front and back)
- Proposed company name in English and Nepali
- Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA) — drafted and signed
- Registered office address proof (rental agreement or ownership document)
- Passport-size photos of all promoters
- Director consent letters from each director

---

## Step 1 — Create Your CAMIS Account

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Username | Choose a unique username (lowercase, no spaces) |
| Email Address | Active email — all notifications and query alerts sent here |
| Mobile Number | Active Nepal number for OTP verification |
| Password | Strong password — save it securely |

> After registration, verify your account via the confirmation link sent to your email before proceeding.

---

## Step 2 — Name Reservation Form Fields

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Proposed Name (English) | Full company name in English — e.g., "Himalayan Tech Solutions Private Limited" |
| Proposed Name (Nepali) | Same name in Devanagari script — e.g., "हिमालयन टेक सोलुसन्स प्राइभेट लिमिटेड" |
| Business Objectives | Select from the predefined list — choose the primary objective accurately |
| Alternative Name 1 | First backup name (in case primary is rejected) |
| Alternative Name 2 | Second backup name |

**Name Rules Checklist:**
- [ ] Unique — not similar to any registered company
- [ ] Submitted in both English and Nepali
- [ ] Does not contain restricted words (Nepal, Bank, University, Government, Royal, National, Insurance) without separate approval
- [ ] Not too generic or vague
- [ ] Ends with "Private Limited" (Pvt. Ltd.) for private companies
- [ ] Ends with "Limited" for public companies

---

## Step 3 — Company Registration Application Fields

### Section A — Company Information

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Exactly as approved in Step 2 — copy character by character |
| Company Type | Private Limited / Public Limited / Profit Not-Distributing / etc. |
| Registered Office Address | Full address: Province, District, Municipality, Ward No., Tole, Street |
| Office Phone | Company phone number |
| Company Email | Company email address |
| Business Objectives | Primary and secondary objectives (must match MOA exactly) |

### Section B — Capital Structure

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Authorized Capital (NPR) | Maximum capital company is legally allowed — as stated in MOA |
| Paid-Up Capital (NPR) | Amount actually being invested at registration — at least NPR 1,00,000 |
| Number of Shares | Total number of shares (Authorized Capital ÷ Face Value Per Share) |
| Face Value Per Share (NPR) | Typically NPR 100 or NPR 1,000 |

> Example: Authorized Capital NPR 10,00,000 ÷ Face Value NPR 100 = 10,000 total shares

### Section C — Shareholder / Promoter Details (Repeat for Each Shareholder)

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Exactly as in citizenship certificate |
| Citizenship Number | Full number with all dashes and slashes |
| Issue District | District where citizenship was issued |
| Issue Date | Date citizenship was issued |
| Permanent Address | As in citizenship certificate |
| Number of Shares Held | Shares this person holds (total across all shareholders must equal paid-up shares) |
| Percentage of Shareholding | Each shareholder's % (all must total 100%) |
| Contact Number | Phone number |
| Email | Email address |

### Section D — Director Details (Repeat for Each Director)

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Exactly as in citizenship certificate |
| Citizenship Number | Full number |
| Designation | Chairman / Managing Director / Director |
| Date of Appointment | Date they are being appointed as director |
| Contact Number | Phone number |

> A shareholder and a director can be the same person. For single-person companies, the same individual is listed in both sections.

## Step 4 — Document Upload Guide

| Document | Format | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Application Form (Anusuchi 1) | PDF | With NPR 5 revenue stamp affixed |
| MOA (Prabandha Patra) | PDF | 2 signed copies — both pages clearly visible |
| AOA (Niyamawali) | PDF | 2 signed copies — both pages clearly visible |
| Citizenship Certificate (all promoters) | PDF / JPEG | Front and back on same page or separate pages — clear and legible |
| Registered Office Proof | PDF / JPEG | Rental agreement or ownership document with address matching the application |
| Passport-size Photos | JPEG | All promoters — white background, recent |
| Director Consent Letters | PDF | Signed by each director individually |
| Mutual Agreement | PDF | If multiple promoters — signed by all |

**Upload Rules:**
- Accepted formats: PDF, JPEG
- File size: within the portal's specified limit — compress if too large
- Quality: every character must be readable — no blurry, dark, or cut-off scans
- Citizenship back must be included — front-only uploads are rejected

---

## MOA (Prabandha Patra) — Key Sections to Include

The Memorandum of Association must contain these sections:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Exact OCR-approved name |
| Registered Office | Full address matching the application |
| Business Objectives | All planned activities — primary and secondary |
| Liability | "Limited" — liability of shareholders is limited to their investment |
| Authorized Capital | Total authorized capital in NPR and number of shares |
| Share Structure | Number and type of shares, face value per share |
| Promoters | Full names, citizenship numbers, addresses, and shares held by each promoter |
| Signatures | Signed by all promoters with date |

---

## AOA (Niyamawali) — Key Sections to Include

The Articles of Association must cover:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Company Name and Type | Same as MOA |
| Share Transfer Rules | Conditions under which shares can be transferred between shareholders |
| Board of Directors | Minimum and maximum number, appointment and removal procedures |
| Board Meeting Rules | Notice period, quorum requirements, voting procedures |
| General Meeting Rules | AGM timing, notice period, quorum, shareholder voting rights |
| Managing Director | Role, powers, and appointment process |
| Accounts and Audit | Financial year, auditor appointment, financial statement requirements |
| Dividend Policy | How profits may be distributed |
| Amendment Procedure | How MOA and AOA can be amended in future |

---

## Corrections Policy

| Stage | Can You Correct? |
|---|---|
| Before submitting the online application | Yes — edit freely in CAMIS |
| After submission, before OCR review | Log in to CAMIS and request withdrawal/correction if the portal allows |
| After OCR raises a query | Respond through CAMIS within 7 days — upload corrected documents |
| After Certificate of Incorporation is issued | File a formal amendment through CAMIS (name, address, directors, capital changes each follow separate amendment processes) |

## Pre-Submission Checklist

**Name Reservation:**
- [ ] Company name prepared in both English and Nepali
- [ ] Name checked against naming rules (no restricted words, not too generic)
- [ ] 2–3 backup names prepared

**Application Form:**
- [ ] Company name matches OCR-approved name exactly (character by character)
- [ ] Business objectives match those stated in MOA exactly
- [ ] Authorized capital and share structure are consistent between application and MOA
- [ ] All shareholder percentages total exactly 100%
- [ ] Registered office address matches the uploaded office proof document

**Documents:**
- [ ] MOA — 2 copies, signed by all promoters, clear scan
- [ ] AOA — 2 copies, signed by all promoters, clear scan
- [ ] Citizenship certificates — front and back, all promoters, clearly readable
- [ ] Registered office proof — address matches application
- [ ] Revenue stamp (NPR 5) affixed to application form
- [ ] Director consent letters — signed by each director
- [ ] Passport-size photos — all promoters

**Before Clicking Submit:**
- [ ] Review every field on the confirmation page
- [ ] Confirm all uploaded documents are correct versions (not an old draft)
- [ ] Set up email notifications from CAMIS for query alerts

---

## Post-Submission Monitoring

After submitting:
1. Monitor your CAMIS dashboard daily for OCR queries
2. OCR query response deadline: 7 days from query notice
3. Payment notice appears when OCR approves documents — pay immediately through the CAMIS payment gateway
4. After payment, download your Certificate of Incorporation from the CAMIS dashboard
5. Proceed immediately to PAN registration at IRD (within 7 days mandatory)

---

## Important Tips

- Fill all fields while keeping your citizenship certificate open in front of you — names and numbers must match exactly
- The company name in the MOA must be an exact copy of the OCR-approved name — even a comma or spacing difference causes rejection
- Business objectives in the application must match objectives in the MOA — any mismatch is flagged as a query
- Authorized capital entered in the application and in the MOA must be identical
- All shareholder equity percentages must add up to exactly 100%
- Keep all original documents safe after registration — you will need them for bank account opening, PAN registration, and future amendments
