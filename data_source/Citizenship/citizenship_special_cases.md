# Citizenship Special Cases — Nepal

---

## Overview

Certain applicants require additional documents or follow a different process. Use the sections below to find your situation.

---

## Special Case Document Requirements

| Situation | Extra Documents Required |
|---|---|
| One parent is deceased | Death certificate of deceased parent. If their citizenship unavailable: voter ID, family registration, or local authority testimony |
| Father's identity unknown or unestablished | SoGhoshana (Sworn Declaration) signed by both applicant and mother before the CDO |
| Applicant living in a different district from parental citizenship | Migration Certificate (Basha Sarai Praman-Patra) from Ward Office of previous permanent address |
| Married woman adding husband's name or address | Marriage registration certificate + Ward Office Sifarish from husband's ward |
| Divorced woman reverting to maiden name | Divorce certificate (Sambandha Viched Praman-Patra) + Ward Office recommendation |
| Foreign woman married to Nepali man | Valid marriage registration certificate + proof of initiating foreign citizenship renunciation |
| Child of Nepali mother and foreign father | Permanent residence proof + no foreign citizenship + SoGhoshana |
| Orphan children | Institution recommendation + local government Surjaamin (identity verification) |
| Applicant with special contribution (science/arts/sports) | Proof of 15+ years Nepal residence + language proficiency + renunciation of foreign citizenship |

## Mother-Based Citizenship (Father Unidentified) — 2082 Amendment

This is the most significant legal change under the Nepal Citizenship Second Amendment Act 2082 (effective Ashoj 5, 2082).

**Who qualifies:**
- Child born to a Nepali mother
- Father's identity cannot be established (unknown, unidentified, or absent)

**Process:**
1. Collect citizenship application form from the Ward Office
2. Prepare documents (see below)
3. Submit to Ward Office for Sifarish Patra
4. Visit the DAO with the mother
5. Both applicant and mother sign a SoGhoshana (Sworn Declaration) before the CDO confirming the father's identity is unknown
6. CDO reviews and issues citizenship by descent through the mother

**Required Documents:**
- Birth certificate of the applicant
- Mother's citizenship certificate
- Ward Office recommendation (Sifarish Patra)
- SoGhoshana signed by both applicant and mother (format: Schedule 7-Kha under the Fourth Amendment Rules 2082)
- 3–4 passport-size photographs

> Under Section 8-Ka of the amended Act, the applicant may also request that one parent's name be omitted from the certificate.

## Married Women — Special Cases

### Option 1: Retain Father's Name (Maiden Citizenship Unchanged)
- No additional documents needed beyond standard process
- Citizenship remains in the applicant's maiden name with parents' address

### Option 2: Update Citizenship with Husband's Name After Marriage
- Marriage registration certificate
- Ward Office Sifarish from husband's ward area
- Submit to the DAO with original citizenship for update

### Option 3: Divorced Woman Reverting to Maiden Name
- Divorce certificate (Sambandha Viched Praman-Patra)
- Ward Office recommendation letter
- Submit to DAO to revert to maiden name and parents' address

## Foreign Woman Married to a Nepali Male

Eligible for naturalized citizenship under the Nepal Citizenship Act.

**Required Documents:**
- Valid marriage registration certificate
- Proof of initiating renunciation of foreign citizenship
- Copy of foreign citizenship certificate or passport
- Ward Office Sifarish
- Passport-size photographs (4 copies)
- Application form

**Process:**
1. Obtain Ward Office Sifarish
2. Submit all documents at the DAO
3. DAO may request police verification
4. CDO reviews and issues naturalized citizenship

**Processing Time:** 1–2 weeks

---

## Applying from a Different District

If your current permanent address differs from where your parents' citizenship was issued:

- Bring a Migration Certificate (Basha Sarai Praman-Patra) from the Ward Office of your previous permanent address
- Your current Ward Office issues the Sifarish Patra
- Your current DAO issues the citizenship certificate
- You do not need to travel back to the original district if you have the migration certificate

---

## Children Born Abroad to Nepali Parents

Children born abroad may apply for citizenship in Nepal through descent if at least one parent is a Nepali citizen.

**If the child has no Minor Identity Card (born abroad and never registered in Nepal):**
1. Contact the nearest Nepali embassy or consulate in the country of residence
2. Register the birth at the embassy — obtain a birth registration or temporary registration number
3. Use this number in the citizenship application process upon returning to Nepal
4. Obtain a Minor Identity Card from the local Ward Office first
5. Then apply for citizenship at the DAO from age 16

**Documents needed:**
- Copies of both parents' Nepali citizenship certificates
- Copies of both parents' passports
- Parents' marriage registration certificate
- Child's original birth registration certificate
- Ward Office recommendation

## Single Parent Cases

### Deceased Father — Application Still Possible
- Provide the father's death certificate
- Mother accompanies as the verifying parent (Sanaakhat)
- CDO has authority to accept supporting documents if the father's original citizenship records are unavailable
- Ward Office testimony or local authority verification may be accepted

### Both Parents Unavailable for Sanaakhat
- A close relative who is a Nepali citizen may accompany the applicant
- CDO may initiate police verification (Sarjaamin) for additional confirmation
- Processing may take 2–5 additional working days

---

## Dual Citizenship at Birth — Choice Rule

If a person acquires both Nepali and another country's citizenship at birth:
- They must formally choose which to retain **before age 18**
- The window is within 2 years from their 16th birthday
- If no formal choice is made, Nepali citizenship lapses automatically
- Choice must be declared at the DAO

---

## Re-Acquisition of Citizenship

Former Nepali citizens who voluntarily renounced citizenship and later renounce their foreign citizenship may re-apply.

**Under Section 11 of the Nepal Citizenship Act:**
1. Obtain official proof of renunciation of foreign citizenship
2. Apply at the DAO with all supporting documents
3. DAO processes the re-acquisition application

---

## NRN (Non-Resident Nepali) Citizenship

A special legal status for individuals of Nepali origin who have acquired foreign citizenship in a non-SAARC country.

**Eligibility:**
- Persons of Nepali origin residing outside SAARC countries
- Must hold foreign citizenship of a non-SAARC country
- Father, mother, grandfather, or grandmother must have been a Nepali citizen at some point

**Rights granted under NRN Citizenship:**
- Economic, social, and cultural rights within Nepal
- May purchase land (limited — up to 2 Ropani in Kathmandu Valley, 8 Kattha in Terai municipalities, 10 Ropani in other areas, subject to MoFA prior permission)
- Does NOT include: political rights, voting rights, administrative rights, or the right to hold government office

**Step-by-Step Process:**

**Part A — Renunciation of Nepali Citizenship (if previously held)**
1. Visit the DAO where original Nepali citizenship was issued
2. Collect the renunciation form (original Nepali paper — no photocopies accepted)
3. Submit: original Nepali citizenship certificate + original Nepali passport + copy of foreign citizenship/passport + photos
4. DAO writes to the police for a background check
5. Police investigation and clearance
6. DAO issues a Citizenship Renunciation Confirmation Letter

**Part B — NRN Citizenship Application**
1. Collect application form from Ward Office or DAO
2. Obtain Ward Office recommendation (Sifarish Patra) confirming familial/ancestral connection to Nepal
3. Submit complete application at the DAO in Nepal or Nepali embassy/consulate abroad
4. At least 2 close relatives (or Nepali citizens who know the applicant) must appear in person at the DAO for identity verification
5. CDO reviews documents; may initiate police background check if needed
6. Applicant takes an oath affirming allegiance to Nepal's Constitution
7. NRN Citizenship Certificate is issued

**Documents Required for NRN Citizenship:**
- Completed NRN citizenship application form
- Ward Office recommendation letter (Sifarish Patra)
- Citizenship Renunciation Confirmation Letter (if previously Nepali citizen)
- Copy of foreign citizenship certificate
- Copy of valid foreign passport
- Copy of NRN Identity Card (if already held)
- Copies of Nepali citizenship of father, mother, or grandparent (proving ancestral link)
- Proof of foreign residence (utility bill, tenancy contract, etc.)
- Commitment letter to abide by Nepali law (format available at the DAO)
- Recent passport-size photographs (prepare 8–10 copies — multiple offices require them)
- Police Clearance Certificate (from country of residence)

**Processing Time:**
- Direct application at DAO in Nepal: **10–12 working days**
- Application through embassy abroad: significantly longer due to forwarding and procedural delays — DAO application in Nepal is generally recommended

> **Note:** Embassy-based applications are currently not preferred due to delays. Most applicants are redirected to Nepal for processing.

## Citizenship Correction (Error in Issued Certificate)

If there is an error in your citizenship certificate (wrong name, date, address):

- Apply with the original citizenship certificate and documents proving the correct entry
- Submit at the same DAO that issued the original certificate
- If the error is the DAO's fault: correction may be made at no additional fee
- If the error is the applicant's fault: standard processing applies
- Bring supporting documents (birth certificate, academic certificates) that show the correct information

## Termination / Loss of Citizenship

Nepali citizenship is automatically lost in these situations:

| Situation | Legal Basis |
|---|---|
| Voluntarily acquiring foreign citizenship | Section 10 — automatic loss from that date, no court proceeding required |
| Obtaining citizenship by fraud or misrepresentation | Citizenship may be cancelled by order |
| Dual nationals at birth who do not declare their choice before age 18 | Nepali citizenship lapses automatically |

---

## Common Rejection Reasons (What to Avoid)

| Issue | How to Fix |
|---|---|
| Birth registration mismatch (name/DOB differs from parental citizenship) | Fix the birth record at the Local Registrar FIRST, then file citizenship |
| Filing at wrong DAO (not your permanent address district) | Apply at the DAO covering your permanent address as in parental citizenship |
| Missing Ward Office Sifarish | Always obtain Sifarish first — DAO will not accept files without it |
| Incomplete documents | Verify the complete checklist at your specific district DAO before filing |
| Applicant's name inconsistency across documents | Ensure name matches exactly across birth certificate, parental citizenship, and academic certificates |
| Parent not present for Sanaakhat | Arrange for at least one parent or close relative to accompany you |
