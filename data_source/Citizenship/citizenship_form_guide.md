# Citizenship Application Form Guide — Nepal

---

## Overview

The citizenship application form (Schedule 1 Form) is collected **free of charge** from your local Ward Office. Fill it yourself for accuracy. Cross-check every field against your birth certificate and parents' citizenship certificate before submitting.

The form is available at your Ward Office counter and at the DAO. There is no online submission — the form must be submitted in person.

---

## Form Field Reference

| # | Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Full Name (Surname)** | Exactly as in your birth certificate and parents' citizenship. Include all surnames. No titles (Mr., Ms., Dr.) |
| 2 | **Full Name (Given Name)** | All names except surname, including middle name |
| 3 | **Date of Birth (B.S.)** | Enter directly from your birth certificate (Janma Darta Praman-Patra) |
| 4 | **Date of Birth (A.D.)** | Convert from B.S. using an online calendar converter |
| 5 | **Place of Birth** | District name if born in Nepal. Country name if born abroad |
| 6 | **Sex / Gender** | M (Male) · F (Female) · Other — as per your birth certificate |
| 7 | **Permanent Address** | Province, District, Municipality, Ward No., Tole — as per parental citizenship or migration certificate |
| 8 | **Father's Full Name** | Exactly as in your father's citizenship certificate |
| 9 | **Mother's Full Name** | Exactly as in your mother's citizenship certificate |
| 10 | **Father's Citizenship No.** | Citizenship number from father's certificate |
| 11 | **Mother's Citizenship No.** | Citizenship number from mother's certificate |
| 12 | **Grandfather's Name** | As available in family records or parental citizenship |
| 13 | **Grandmother's Name** | As available in family records or parental citizenship |
| 14 | **Applicant's Signature / Thumbprint** | Sign in the designated box. Thumbprint for those unable to sign |
| 15 | **Date of Application** | The date you are submitting the form |

---

## Name Entry — Critical Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Use exact spelling | Name must match exactly across birth certificate, academic certificate, and parental citizenship |
| Include all surnames | If you have multiple surnames or compound surnames, include all |
| No dots in abbreviations | E.g., write GC not G.C. |
| No titles | Do not write Mr., Mrs., Dr., or similar |
| Middle name goes in Given Name field | Do not put middle name in the Surname field |
| Father unidentified (2082 Amendment) | If applying through mother only, leave Father fields as directed by the CDO; a SoGhoshana is required |

---

## Address Entry Rules

| Situation | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Living in same district as parental citizenship | Use address as shown in parental citizenship |
| Relocated to a new district | Use new address + attach Migration Certificate (Basha Sarai Praman-Patra) |
| Married woman with new address | Use new address + attach Marriage Certificate |

---

## Documents to Attach with the Form

Attach **photocopies** of all documents below. Bring **originals** for verification — they will be checked and returned.

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Birth Certificate (Janma Darta) | Most important — original + photocopy |
| Father's Citizenship Certificate | Photocopy + original for verification |
| Mother's Citizenship Certificate | Photocopy + original for verification |
| Academic Certificate (SLC/SEE or above) | To verify name spelling and date of birth |
| Migration Certificate | Only if address differs from parental citizenship district |
| Marriage Certificate | Only for married female applicants updating address or name |
| Divorce Certificate | Only if reverting to maiden name after divorce |
| Death Certificate | Only if one parent is deceased |
| SoGhoshana | Only for cases where father's identity is unknown |
| Passport-Size Photos | 3–4 copies, 35mm x 45mm, white background |

## Ward Office Step — What Happens

Before submitting the form to the DAO, the Ward Office must:

1. Verify the applicant's identity
2. Confirm permanent residence
3. Confirm parental relationship
4. Issue the **Sifarish Patra** (recommendation letter)

The Sifarish Patra processing time: same day to 1–2 working days.

> The DAO will not accept any citizenship application without the Ward Office Sifarish Patra. This step cannot be skipped.

---

## DAO Step — What Happens

After receiving your Ward Office Sifarish:

1. Book an online token appointment at your DAO's website (required in Kathmandu and most major districts)
2. Visit on your appointment date with:
   - Completed application form
   - All original documents and photocopies
   - Sifarish Patra from Ward Office
   - 3–4 passport-size photographs
   - At least one parent (for Sanaakhat / identity verification)
3. DAO staff verify all documents at the counter
4. CDO or authorized officer reviews and signs
5. Certificate is issued (same day if documents are complete)

---

## Corrections Policy

| Stage | Can You Correct? |
|---|---|
| Before submitting to Ward Office | Yes — edit the form yourself |
| After Ward Office Sifarish, before DAO submission | Yes — return to Ward Office if changes are needed |
| At DAO counter before CDO signature | Yes — request correction from the officer |
| After citizenship is issued | ❌ No — must apply for a correction/duplicate with supporting documents |

> If the error is the DAO's fault, correction is made free of charge. If the error is the applicant's fault, standard processing applies.

---

## Date Conversion

All dates on Nepali government documents are in **Bikram Sambat (B.S.)**. The application form requires both A.D. and B.S. dates.

- Use an official B.S. to A.D. converter (available on Nepal government websites and public calendar tools)
- Your birth certificate will show the B.S. date — convert it to A.D. for the form
- Ensure the converted A.D. date matches your academic certificates exactly

---

## Checklist Before Submitting to Ward Office

- [ ] Form filled with ink (no pencil), no overwriting or corrections with correction fluid
- [ ] Full name matches birth certificate and parents' citizenship exactly
- [ ] Date of birth (B.S. and A.D.) matches birth certificate
- [ ] Permanent address matches parental citizenship (or migration certificate attached)
- [ ] Father's and mother's full names match their citizenship certificates exactly
- [ ] Photocopies of all required documents attached
- [ ] 3–4 passport-size photographs (35mm x 45mm, white background) attached
- [ ] At least one parent is ready to accompany you to the DAO
- [ ] Migration certificate attached (if applicable)
- [ ] Marriage/divorce/death certificate attached (if applicable)

---

## Important Tips

- Fill the form yourself — if someone helps you, review every field before submitting
- Do not leave required fields blank — the Ward Office and DAO will send you back to complete them
- Academic certificate (SLC/SEE) is not a substitute for the birth certificate — both may be required
- Bring more photos than you think you need — different offices may each require copies
- If you are unsure about any field, ask the Ward Office staff before submitting — corrections after issuance require a full re-application
