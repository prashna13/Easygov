# Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for a PAN Card

A **Permanent Account Number (PAN)** is a unique 10-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Income Tax Department of India. It is essential for financial transactions, filing income tax returns, opening bank accounts, and investing.

---

## 1. Types of PAN Application Forms

| Form Type | Who Should Use It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Form 49A** | Indian Citizens, Entities incorporated in India, and Unincorporated entities |
| **Form 49AA** | Foreign Citizens, Entities incorporated outside India |

---

## 2. Methods to Apply

You can apply through one of the following methods:

### Method A: Instant e-PAN (Free & Paperless — Fastest)
* **Eligibility:** Indian citizens with a valid Aadhaar linked to an active mobile number.
* **Cost:** Free of cost (digital copy generated in PDF format).
* **Portal:** [Income Tax e-Filing Portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
* **Steps:**
  1. Go to **Quick Links** > **Instant e-PAN**.
  2. Click on **Get New e-PAN**.
  3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and confirm.
  4. Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  5. Validate details and submit. Your e-PAN is generated within minutes.

---

### Method B: Online Application for Physical Card (via Protean / UTIITSL)
* **Official Portals:** 
  * [Protean (formerly NSDL e-Gov)](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endConsumerRegisterOnLine.html)
  * [UTIITSL Portal](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com)
* **Processing Fee:** 
  * Physical card delivery within India: ~₹107 (including GST)
  * Physical card delivery outside India: ~₹1,017 (including GST)

#### Step-by-Step Online Process:
1. **Register Request:** Select Application Type (**Form 49A**) and Category (Individual, Firm, etc.).
2. **Fill Details:** Enter personal details (Name, DOB, Email, Mobile number) to generate a **Token Number**.
3. **Select Submission Mode:**
   * **e-KYC & e-Sign (Paperless):** Uses Aadhaar OTP (no document upload needed; photo taken from Aadhaar).
   * **Submit scanned images via e-Sign:** Upload scanned photo, signature, and documents.
   * **Forward application documents physically:** Print the form, attach photos/signatures, and courier to Protean/UTIITSL office.
4. **Enter Contact & Parent Details:** Provide address, father's/mother's name, and AO (Assessing Officer) Code (searchable by city on the portal).
5. **Upload Documents:** ID proof, address proof, and date of birth proof (if not using direct Aadhaar e-KYC).
6. **Payment & Submission:** Pay online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
7. **Acknowledgement:** Save the 15-digit acknowledgement slip for tracking.

---

## 3. Documents Required

| Category | Accepted Documents |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Identity Proof (POI)** | Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence, Ration Card with photo |
| **Address Proof (POA)** | Aadhaar Card, Passport, Utility Bill (Electricity/Water/Gas < 3 months old), Bank Statement |
| **Date of Birth Proof (DOB)** | Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, Matriculation/10th Marksheet, Passport |

*(Note: Aadhaar serves as a single valid proof for Identity, Address, and DOB).*

---

## 4. Tracking Your Application Status

* **Protean (NSDL):** Visit the [Protean PAN Tracking Page](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html) and enter your 15-digit Acknowledgement Number.
* **UTIITSL:** Visit the [UTIITSL PAN Tracking Page](https://www.trackpan.utiitsl.com/) and enter your Application Coupon Number.
* **Delivery:** Once approved, physical cards are dispatched by India Post and typically delivered within 7–15 working days.

---

## 5. Key Tips & Precautions

1. **Aadhaar-PAN Name Match:** Ensure your name and date of birth in the application match your Aadhaar record exactly to avoid rejection.
2. **Signature Quality:** If uploading a signature, sign clearly on plain white paper with a dark black or blue pen.
3. **Mandatory Linking:** Under current tax regulations, linking your PAN with Aadhaar is mandatory for all individual taxpayers.
